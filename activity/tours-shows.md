# ⛴️ Tours/Shows

## Unbridled at Untitled

The Unbridled show is a burlesque/variety show that Untitled Supper Club hosts every Thursday. The shows change and sport new cast members and acts regularly.&#x20;

Location: River North

Budget: $5 entertainment fee + expected spend on food and drinks

{% embed url="https://untitledsupperclub.com/unbridled/" %}

## Chicago Crime Tour

The Chicago Crime tour takes you around in a charter bus to tell you about Chicago's rich crime history. This is the tour that makes it possible for you to be the person in your friend group who says things like "did you know about the Tylenol murders?" when you pass a Walgreens.

Location: Streeterville

Budget: $42 per person

[https://chicagocrimetours.com/](https://chicagocrimetours.com/)

## Second City

Second City is a premier improv comedy troupe that has been based and continually operating in Chicago since the 1950s. The troupe has had graduates who went on to become comedy staples and major SNL performers. John Belushi, Tina Fey, and Mike Myers are notable alumni.

Location: Old Town

Budget: $50

{% embed url="https://www.secondcity.com/" %}

## Chicago Architecture Tour

Chicago has a fascinating architectural history, and the Chicago Architecture Foundation is here to tell you all about it. It's about 1.5 hours and goes up and down the downtown portion of the Chicago river. There are several river tours, but make sure to do the Chicago Architecture Foundation tours as they do an incredible job. An additional $5 gets you admission to the Chicago Architecture Center. :star: :fire:

Location: Loop

Budget: $57 per person + fees.

{% embed url="https://www.architecture.org/tours/detail/chicago-architecture-foundation-center-river-cruise-aboard-chicago-s-first-lady/#tickets" %}
